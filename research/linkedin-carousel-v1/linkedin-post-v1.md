I built a midterm tracker this month on one bet, that the polls are still missing Democrats the way they did last fall, and this week I tried to make it hold up in front of a statistician.

The first version of the odds treated every race as its own coin flip, and it said Democrats were 99% to control the Senate. Polls don't miss like that. When they're off, they're off in the same direction almost everywhere (in 2020, 35 of 38 Senate and governor races missed the same way), so I drew the misses together, ran 20,000 elections, and the Senate came back at 91%. Then I replayed every polled Senate and governor race from 2010 to 2024. Counting races one at a time missed the real seat count in 3 of 8 years. Drawing them together missed none.

I also didn't trust my Iowa number. Checking it turned up a real bug in how I read early voting, and I fixed it for every state at once. The rest held up. Trump won Iowa by 13, the national swing is about 11, and that puts Iowa right about where I have it.

It freezes Monday, November 2, and gets graded against the results.
